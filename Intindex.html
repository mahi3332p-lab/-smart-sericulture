🌱 About Sericulture

Sericulture is the scientific process of rearing silkworms for the production of silk. It includes mulberry cultivation, silkworm rearing, cocoon harvesting and silk processing.

🌳 Mulberry Cultivation

Soil

Mulberry grows well in fertile, well-drained soil.

Irrigation

Provide suitable irrigation according to soil and weather conditions.

Fertilizers

Use soil-test-based organic and recommended fertilizers.

Plant Protection

Monitor pests and diseases regularly and follow safe management.

🐛 Silkworm Rearing

Egg Stage

Silkworm life begins from eggs.

Larval Stage

Silkworm larvae feed on fresh mulberry leaves.

Chrysalis / Pupa

The silkworm develops inside the cocoon into a pupa.

Moth Stage

An adult moth emerges from the cocoon.

📚 Important Sericulture Practices

  • Maintain a clean rearing room.
  • Provide fresh and suitable mulberry leaves.
  • Maintain recommended temperature and humidity.
  • Clean rearing equipment regularly.
  • Observe silkworm health every day.
  • Harvest mature cocoons at the correct time.
